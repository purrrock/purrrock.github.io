The Cardboard Castle King

Альбом "Диорама" (2025)

On a dusty old rug, by the mountains of books, I built up a castle, just see how it looks! My throne is of pillows, my crown is of foil, No thunder can shake it, no blizzard can spoil. It may not be marble with pearls shining bright, But my cardboard fortress is built upon might! I’m the King! My castle — a world without ends! From mice to the birds, every creature’s a friend! My kingdom is born from a fanciful thought, A home to free people — the treasure I’ve sought! And though some may say, “It’s an empty old box!” My imagination is the key that unlocks! From food bowls I built a rampart on the floor, To keep the dust-monster away from my door. I fight with a dragon (a sock, to be fair!), My moat in the bathroom makes everyone stare! The roach-ambassadors I welcome with grace, Then squash them if tribute is not in its place! I’m the King! And my castle’s a world you can’t see! Where everyone’s happy and playing with me! My kingdom is built out of laughter and dreams, My royal edicts are as real as it seems! And though some may scoff, “It is merely paper and glue,” But here I am Lord — my law is steady and true! You’re right, little Pips, the true world is inside. More precious than treasure are the sparks in your eyes. They shine like the stars, lighting up every way, The strongest of walls are in your head, they stay. No storm can destroy them, no rain makes them fall, For in your own world, you’re the lord of it all. They say that true riches are things you can hold, That joy can be purchased with silver and gold. My castle is cardboard, yet rich is my stay, I’m glad for this fortress with each passing day! Here everyone plays their own purpose and role, Just trust in your power and play from the soul! I am King! My castle — my world never ends! I don’t need the gold, nor pompous royal trends! My kingdom is born from a fanciful thought, Where hearts can sing freely and never be caught! And though some may say, "It's an empty old crate!" It holds all my faith, and my beauty, and fate! A box? No, my friend, it is truly a throne! The King is who laughs, and who rules on his own! Purrrrrrr...
